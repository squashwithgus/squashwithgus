The Purist Doctrine

Built on Suffocating Length, Kinetic Deceleration & Relentless Retrieval.

Between 1998 and 2004, Gustavo dominated the Balearic racket circuit, capturing the Mallorca Regional Championship four consecutive times. His game was built around punishing lung capacity, suffocating depth, and total spatial ownership of the T.

That international trajectory ended abruptly at age 27 during an exhibition semifinal in Barcelona. Chasing a dying boast into the front-left nick, his left shoe caught high-traction polyurethane varnish; his foot anchored while his torso rotated. The result was a catastrophic multi-ligament rupture (complete ACL and lateral meniscus tear).

Relocating to Munich for specialized orthopedic rehabilitation, Gus transformed injury into biomechanical science. His curriculum is not designed for recreational chit-chat. It is an uncompromising breakdown of joint-preserving deceleration, wrist discipline, and uncompromising shot depth.

Kinetic Joint Preservation

Re-architecting the 5-step recovery lunge to eliminate rotational shear on the patella and meniscus, born directly from Gus's personal post-surgical rehabilitation.

The 50-Drive Mandate

You will strike 50 consecutive backhand drives within 5cm of the nick behind the service box before Gus permits you to even glance at a drop shot.

Clarity & Rules

Frequently Asked Questions

Normally, I do not waste time teaching basic racket grips. However, beginners may now apply for an Introductory Trial on an exclusive case-by-case basis. Whether you receive ongoing coaching is determined immediately following the trial round, assessed strictly on the overall aesthetic attractiveness and visual appeal of the player on court. If accepted, bring your own racket — do not ask to borrow mine.
You pay me to rebuild your mechanics, not to be your sparring partner. We will play targeted condition games to test your tactical pressure, but if you simply want to run around and hit the tin, join a casual club ladder.
Padel is beach tennis inside a chicken coop. It is a recreational activity for people who refuse to sweat or learn proper rotational footwork. Seeing those cages take up valuable hardwood floor space in Munich drives me to quiet fury. Do not ask me this again.
Strictly indoor non-marking squash shoes (gum rubber or specialized non-marking court soles). If you step onto the hardwood in outdoor running shoes or street sneakers, you will not set foot on court.
