## 🚀 Features & Easter Eggs
- **Photorealistic Coach Portraits:** High-resolution 35mm editorial sports portraits of Gus inside a glass-back squash court with authentic documentary texture, lighting, and posture.
- **The Coach's Journal (Cuaderno de Pista):** In-depth technical blog and dispatches based directly on Gus's backstory (geometry of the straight rail, 2008 Barcelona ACL tear autopsy, scouting analysis of his estranged son Mateo, and post-match Paulaner Spezi protocols).
- **Progressive Depth & Irony Architecture:** Serious, authoritative, prestigious European coaching landing page that progressively reveals Gus's hilarious purist obsessions, family feud, and secret burner surveillance as visitors engage more deeply.
- **Interactive Long-Form Dispatch Reader:** Modal reader with typography tailored for technical coaching dispatches.
- **Web Audio API Synth:** Authentic squash ball *thwack* and metallic *tin* sound effects without external audio dependencies.
- **Escalating Telemetry & Diagnostics:** Progressive diagnostic tool and 3-question mechanical audit that filter out padel players.
- **Mateo's Burner Surveillance Log:** Secret modal containing Gus's anonymous burner account (`@purist_balearic_04`) notes on his son Mateo's Premier Padel tournament matches.
- **Interactive Booking Application:** Instant screening of candidates (with immediate rejection if Padel or beginner level is selected).
- **Fully Responsive & Dark Mode Glassmorphism:** Styled with Tailwind CSS, Inter font, and classic squash-court red line patterns.
